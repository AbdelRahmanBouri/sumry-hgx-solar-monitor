How this estimate works

Potential is built in three steps. The sun's position for your coordinates gives clear-sky sunlight on each roof face; the cloud-cover forecast scales it; and the array's own efficiency — the share of that sunlight it has actually delivered at each sun height while the battery could still take charge, over the last week — sets the level. Nothing is hand-tuned: heat, glass reflection at low sun, haze and the string wiring are all in what the array demonstrated. Where the sun climbs higher than the array has yet been free to show, the last learned band is held.

Possible unused solar is the positive gap between modelled potential and inverter-captured PV only when battery voltage indicates the bank is near absorption or float. It can reveal curtailment, but it is not a utility-grade measurement.

Calculator assumes 92% inverter efficiency for AC load and 95% battery charging efficiency. It is a planning tool; actual charger behavior, heat and battery condition will move the result.
