How this estimate works

Potential combines sun position, cloud cover and the array’s 2,640 W nameplate with a system performance factor, projected onto each roof face and reduced for the series mismatch between them.

Possible unused solar is the positive gap between modelled potential and inverter-captured PV only when battery voltage indicates the bank is near absorption or float. It can reveal curtailment, but it is not a utility-grade measurement.

Calculator assumes 92% inverter efficiency for AC load and 95% battery charging efficiency. It is a planning tool; actual charger behavior, heat and battery condition will move the result.
